# 2.0.0-beta.9 Android adapter-off recovery candidate

The owner requested the next release and adoption in the independent examples,
using direct pushes with no PR. Beta.9 contains the Android adapter-off follow-up
at `3f02326`. It retires exact GATT sessions when Android omits its disconnect
callback, closes their clients, settles pending operations and cancelled
connects, and rejects late cleanup from old generations. Restore Bluetooth and
reconnect explicitly. No public API, automatic reconnect, firmware, provisioning,
recording or upload-policy change is introduced.

Beta.8 remains the published baseline during preparation. Its artifacts and
tag, all older releases and npm `latest` must remain unchanged.

## Pre-version phone evidence

Samsung SM-A166U1 / Android 16, SDK-verified Bota Pin serial `4KF6NOHWX0`,
firmware read-back `1.0.19`. Isolated RN and Flutter labs used the examples at
`022edbb`, public beta.8 facade packages and an exclusive local Maven candidate
from source `4d36de5`, CI 36743986531, Android artifact `11112626484`.
AAR SHA-256:
`f8beeb1a97e1e378c616eb83d5c89a46bfc279b15a8d65a57b2409dd8190eaf3`.
Final pre-version source `3f02326`, CI 36748796429, artifact `11114492205`
reproduced that AAR byte-for-byte.

Both apps automatically cleared the connection and disabled status on all three
radio-off cycles. After Bluetooth restoration, scan and the first explicit
reconnect succeeded in each cycle, with the exact serial, firmware and fresh
status. Native logs show close without a disconnect callback on all six losses.
No app restart or manual disconnect was needed between cycles. No GATT 133 was
observed. One initial RN connection timed out after ten seconds, closed its GATT
client and succeeded on a same-session retry; universal first-connect reliability
is not established. Lab-only UI diagnostics exposed the selection address and
firmware; recovery logic was unchanged.

The beta.9 version bump changes native package bytes. These successful candidate
tests therefore do not establish acceptance of the final beta.9 public packages.
Repeat the RN/Flutter public-package recovery checks after publication. Physical
iPhone/macOS/Web, out-of-range, background, other phones/firmware and full recording
workflows remain unverified by this task. The firmware compatibility matrix is
unchanged. Bluetooth was restored on and the labs stopped; wearable data and
lifecycle state were not changed.
